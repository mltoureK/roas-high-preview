First-time setup and written support

What will I receive?

Guidance on connecting the local desktop agent to your web dashboard, the name of your prepared workspace profile, a written opening guide, and a support thread you can return to.

What happens from request to handoff?
  1. Choose email or WhatsApp.
  2. Review the written quote, scope, and billing dates.
  3. If you proceed, follow the workspace invitation and connect your local agent if needed.
  4. Find the profile named in your guide and press Start. Complete any sign-in prompts for your authorized access.
Where is my workspace?

SupportThat can happen. Use your authorized account on the official sign-in page. Keep your password and verification code private. If access is missing, we can check the invitation.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book a call?

No. Quotes, setup steps, and ongoing support happen in writing over email or WhatsApp.

What happens before I pay?

You should receive confirmation of availability, pricing, billing, setup timing, and access conditions in writing before making a payment.

Does Start automatically sign me in?

Start opens the browser profile. The site may still ask you to sign in or verify access.

What does managed setup cover?

The setup covers Multilogin guidance and the assigned browser profile. Any account provisioning and access terms must be confirmed separately in writing.
