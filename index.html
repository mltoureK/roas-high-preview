Setup and written support

What happens from request to handoff?
  1. Choose email or WhatsApp.
  2. Review the written quote, scope, and billing dates.
  3. Accept the workspace invitation. If you’re new to Multilogin, install the desktop app and sign in with the same account.
  4. Find the profile named in your guide and press Start. Complete any sign-in prompts for your authorized access.
