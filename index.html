Inside your setup.

What will I receive?

Guidance on connecting the local agent to your web dashboard, the name of your prepared workspace profile, a written opening guide and a support thread you can return to.

What happens from request to handoff?
  1. Choose email or WhatsApp.
  2. Review the written quote, scope and billing dates.
  3. If you proceed, follow the workspace invitation and connect your local agent if needed.
  4. Find the profile named in your guide and press Start. Complete any sign-in prompts for your authorized access.
Sample support chat · Where is my workspace?

You: I opened the app. Which profile do I use?

Support: Open the workspace named in your invitation. Check the profile name against your guide, then press Start. If it’s missing, tell us which step you reached so we can check your invitation.

Sample support chat · Why am I seeing a sign-in prompt?

You: The browser opened and asked me to sign in.

Support: That can happen. Use your authorized account on the official sign-in page. Keep your password and verification code private. If access is missing, we can check the invitation.

Sample support chat · Where do I add my card?

You: I can’t find the billing settings.

Support: Tell us which business workspace you see. We’ll help you locate its billing area and check whether your role has permission. Enter card details only in the payment form.

A few things to know.

Do I need to book a call?

No. The proposed flow uses email or WhatsApp for questions and setup guidance.

What happens before I pay?

You should receive confirmation of availability, pricing, billing, setup timing and access conditions in writing before making a payment.

Does Start automatically sign me in?

Start opens the browser profile. The site may still ask you to sign in or verify access.

What does managed setup cover?

The setup covers Multilogin guidance and the assigned browser profile. Any account provisioning and access terms must be confirmed separately in writing.
